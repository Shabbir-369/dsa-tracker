# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want DSA Journey Tracker to be a welcoming space for people with different backgrounds, experience levels, identities, and perspectives. Participants should be treated with respect, whether they are reporting an issue, asking a question, reviewing code, or contributing a change.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to feedback.
- Explain disagreements respectfully and focus on evidence and user impact.
- Respect privacy; do not publish someone else's personal information or private project data.
- Accept that people have different levels of experience and may need clarification.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, deliberate intimidation, or repeated disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or attention that is unwelcome in project spaces.
- Knowingly sharing malicious code, credentials, or harmful links.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainer through the private contact/reporting options available on the repository hosting platform. Please include relevant links or context and avoid publicly posting sensitive information.

The maintainer will review reports as fairly and promptly as possible, protect reporter privacy where practical, and may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary. This project is community-run; response times cannot be guaranteed.

## Scope

This code applies in project issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing or participating in the project.

## Acknowledgment

This document is a concise project-specific standard inspired by common open-source community practices. It is not a guarantee that every dispute can be resolved to everyone's satisfaction.
